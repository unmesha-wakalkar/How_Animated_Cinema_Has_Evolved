# Recommended Narrative Sequence

### 01 — HOOK
**“Before animation became an industry, it was an experiment.”**

Immersive hero with a tiny moving mark that grows into a cinematic timeline.

### 02 — THE FIRST MOVEMENTS
Early devices, drawn animation, cut-outs, stop motion and feature experiments.

### 03 — A LANGUAGE OF MOTION
Show animation style and storytelling scale across decades.

### 04 — THE MACHINE CHANGES
Introduce the digital/CGI transition through landmark milestones and selected films.

### 05 — FROM FILM TO INDUSTRY
Studios, production companies, collections/franchises, geography and business evidence.

### 06 — THE STORIES WE KEEP TELLING
Genres, themes and target-audience patterns.

### 07 — ENTER THE ARCHIVE
Search/filter the film universe and open film dossiers.

### 08 — REFLECTION
End with a concise evidence-based statement about what changed, what persisted, and what the data cannot establish.
