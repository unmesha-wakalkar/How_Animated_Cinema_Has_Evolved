# Project Brief

## Title
From Storytelling to Industry

## Subtitle
How Animated Cinema Evolved

## Audience
Visual communication designers, animation/storytelling students, animation enthusiasts, film enthusiasts and curious general audiences.

## Experience goal
Make the visitor feel that they are stepping inside an animated film while learning how animation changed over time. The site is a data story first, an archive second, and a dashboard last.

## Format
Single-page long-form narrative. Use modals/drawers for film dossiers, comparisons, source notes and research media.

## Creative direction
Cinematic minimalism: highly polished typography, immersive full-bleed imagery, restrained UI, strong motion, data-driven transitions and playful micro-interactions.
