# Research Plan

1. Audit the uploaded datasets and identify coverage/quality limitations.
2. Build a normalized master film table and explicit analytical subsets.
3. Add a historical/technology timeline from credible external sources.
4. Explore time, technique, geography, studios, story/genre, reception and business relationships.
5. Test 3–5 narrative hypotheses before selecting one.
6. Build the single-page information architecture and interaction model.
7. Source media conservatively and maintain a visible source/credit layer.

## Research hypothesis discipline
Do not assume the answer is “animation went from art to commercialization.” Treat commercialization as one hypothesis that may or may not be supported.
