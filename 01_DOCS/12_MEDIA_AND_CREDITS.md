# Media & Credits

## Media policy
Prefer media already referenced in the dataset (`Poster_URL`, `Backdrop_URL`) and stable official/reference sources. TMDB documents how image URLs are formed; the app should not hard-code assumptions about undocumented paths.

## Videos
Use YouTube embeds for research clips. Store a source URL/title/channel in the video manifest and provide a visible “Source” link.

## Minimal attribution UI
Place a small “Sources & Credits” text link in the sticky footer and a compact source line inside media modals.

## Important
College use does not automatically remove copyright or platform terms. The implementation should still retain attribution/backlinks and avoid claiming ownership of third-party media.
