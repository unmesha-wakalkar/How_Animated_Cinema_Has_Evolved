# Candidate Story Directions

## A. The Technology Story
How animation techniques changed what creators could put on screen.

**Evidence:** animation style by decade, landmark milestones, selected films.

## B. The Industry Story
How studios, production scale, franchises and global markets changed.

**Evidence:** studio concentration, collections, countries, business subset.

## C. The Storytelling Story
How genres/themes/audience categories shifted over time.

**Evidence:** normalized genre/theme strings, target audience, qualitative context.

## D. Technology × Business
Whether technological shifts coincide with changes in production budgets and box office.

**Evidence:** complete-case budget/box-office subset only.

## E. Global Animation
How different countries/languages/studios contribute to the animation ecosystem.

**Evidence:** country, language, studio, style and genre relationships.

## Selection rule
Run EDA first. Select the narrative whose evidence is both strong and visually communicable. Do not select a winner before validation.
